# MSA Station TMS User Guide

# Welcome to MSA Station TMS

**MSA Station TMS** is the **Transport Management System** operated by **MSA Kargo**. It provides a centralized platform for managing delivery orders, driver dispatch, shipment tracking, and last-mile logistics operations.

---

## 🔐 System Login

To access the MSA Station TMS, open your web browser and navigate to:
`https://tmsmsa.com/msa_station/tms_login.php`

![TMS Login Screen](/img/tms_login_screen.png)

### Login Steps

1. **Email Address:** Enter your registered MSA Kargo email address in the email input field.
2. **Password:** Type your account password in the password field.
3. **Sign In:** Click the **Login** button to authenticate and enter your dashboard.

:::tip Access Issues
If you are unable to log in, contact your system administrator to verify your account credentials or reset your password.
:::
