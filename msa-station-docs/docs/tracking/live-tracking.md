# Live Tracking

The **Live Tracking** module shows the real-time location of your shipments and drivers on an interactive map.

Open it from the sidebar: **Tracking ➔ Live Tracking**.

![Live Tracking Map](/img/tms_live_tracking.png)

---

## 🗺️ Using the Map

The page displays an interactive **map** with markers representing active shipments/drivers.

1. The map loads centered on your active operations area.
2. Each **marker** represents a driver or shipment currently in transit.
3. **Click a marker** to view details about that shipment (such as its booking reference and status).
4. **Zoom and pan** the map to focus on a specific region or route.

:::tip
Live positions depend on the driver app sending location updates. If a marker looks out of date, the driver's device may be offline or hasn't synced recently.
:::

:::info
Tracking status is driven by the updates recorded in [Booking Driver](../driver/booking-driver.md) and the [Update Status](../report-data/update-status.md) screens.
:::
